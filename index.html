About Me

Hello, I’m Henry Xu! I recently graduated from the University of California, Davis with a major in design and a minor in communication. As a graphic and UI/UX designer, I am dedicated to crafting experiences that enhance the people’s lives through clear direction and engagement.

From an early age, I was inspired to learn how to draw so I could share my ideas and bring happiness to others, even taking lessons from a professional artist as a teenager. I became a designer to apply those skills to help people on a wider scale.

As a designer, I believe that hard work and rational decision-making are key to creating successful designs. If you need support in providing rich visual and user experiences to your audience, I am ready to lend a helping hand!
