PIXEL Graphic Design Club — Social Media Branding

PIXEL has been UC Davis's Graphic Design Club since 2005, hosting events to allow graphic designers to grow in their abilities and connect with their peers. In 2026, I had the opportunity to act as PIXEL's visual designer, designing the club's branding, flyers, and social media posts. Below are the brand guidelines I created for 2026 Winter Quarter and Spring Quarter, as well as some of the social media images/flyers I created based on those guidlines.

Winter Quarter Brand Guidelines

My first role as visual designer was to redesign the club's branding for winter. In the transition to the new quarter, we lost a lot of our members, so for our branding I wanted to emphasize a 'new start' for our club. This led me in a more sleek and futuristic direction in contrast to the softer designs of previous years, especially with the main heading font, Ethnocentric. The club's executive helped me refine the design by suggesting we use gradients to enhance the visual quality and themes. Below are some of the posts we made based on those guidelines:

Spring Quarter Brand Guidelines

For spring quarter, we were going to hold PIXEL Pals, an annual where we would work in teams to complete a multi-week project. The theme for the year would be "City Campaign", and I took inspiration from that theme and the general idea of "workshop" to base my designs off of road signs. The main typeface, Oswald, is similar in appearance to the text used for stop signs and other road signs. As per the executive's request, I implemented butterfly assets to connect the designs to spring. Here are some of the posts we made based on the branding:

Key Takeaways

  1. I learned that it's important for social media layouts to not be too restrictive, both for visual variety across many posts and to make it easier for other people to create with.
  2. My early designs started off minimalistic, but the club executive encouraged me to incorporate more color and gradients into my designs to increase their appeal.
  3. For the spring quarter posts, I made sure that text was high enough contrast to be readable on a multi-colored background, using Adobe's Contrast Checker as necessary.

About Me

Hello, I’m Henry Xu! I recently graduated from the University of California, Davis with a major in design and a minor in communication. As a graphic and UI/UX designer, I am dedicated to crafting experiences that enhance the people’s lives through clear direction and engagement.

From an early age, I was inspired to learn how to draw so I could share my ideas and bring happiness to others, even taking lessons from a professional artist as a teenager. I became a designer to apply those skills to help people on a wider scale.

As a designer, I believe that hard work and rational decision-making are key to creating successful designs. If you need support in providing rich visual and user experiences to your audience, I am ready to lend a helping hand!
